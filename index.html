Behind the model

Research architecture

PneumoAI combines a pretrained convolutional network with a bidirectional recurrent network to classify chest X-rays into two research categories.

01 / VISUAL ENCODER

DenseNet121

Extracts a 7 × 7 grid of 1024-dimensional image features from a resized 224 × 224 X-ray.

02 / SPATIAL PROCESSOR

BiLSTM

Processes 49 spatial feature positions as a sequence, then averages their outputs. These are spatial features, not a time series.

03 / CLASSIFICATION

Fully connected layer

Maps the 256-dimensional representation to two logits: NORMAL and PNEUMONIA.

Dataset and limitations

Trained using the public Chest X-ray Pneumonia dataset. Predictions can be wrong, especially on images from other hospitals, populations or acquisition devices. A heatmap is an explanation aid, not a localization ground truth. This application does not replace a radiologist.
